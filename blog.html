STRATEGY GUIDE

Top 5 Deck-Building Strategies in CyberGrid: Tactics

By Marcus Vance
Oct 24, 2026 6 min read

Mastering resource management and counter-drafting rogue factions is essential for climbing high-rank leaderboards in CyberGrid: Tactics. Whether building around early-turn tempo or endgame combo engines, efficiency is key.

1. Establish Early Energy Generators

Prioritize low-cost energy nodes during turns 1 and 2. This guarantees operational bandwidth to field heavy faction units without stalling your drawing phase.

2. Synergize Dual Faction Tags

Mixing more than two factions dilutes card synergies. Stick to dual-faction deck designs to maximize passive stat bonuses and faction-specific trigger abilities.

3. Tech Counter-Cards

Always hold 2-3 disruption routines in your side deck to neutralize opponent combo engines before they snowball in late-game rounds.

HOBBY TUTORIAL

Miniature Dry-Brushing & Washing 101

By Elena Rostova
Oct 18, 2026 8 min read

Dry-brushing and shade washing are two core techniques that quickly elevate your tabletop miniatures from flat plastic models to detailed, table-ready figures.

1. Applying the Shade Wash

After your base coat dries completely, apply an acrylic wash into armor recesses, cloth folds, and facial details to create shadow contrast.

2. Catching the Edges with Dry-Brushing

Use a flat brush with damp-dry paint to catch raised edges across your miniature, highlighting folds and metal surfaces smoothly.

How to Host Your First Co-op Game Night

By David Chen
Oct 12, 2026 5 min read

Hosting a smooth cooperative tabletop night requires selecting accessible titles, pacing rule explanations, and setting up an inviting space for player interaction.

1. Pick the Right Entry-Level Title

Start with accessible cooperative titles so every player can join decision-making without feeling overwhelmed by complex mechanics.

2. Set Up Easy Table Navigation

Keep rulebooks, token trays, and card organizers placed within reach of every chair so turns transition quickly.
