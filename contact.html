Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PlayHaven table bookings, events, and store services.

Do I need to reserve tables in advance, or are walk-ins welcome?

While walk-ins are always welcome for open social brunch tables, we strongly recommend booking ahead for Friday night competitive tournaments and D&D campaign slots to guarantee your seat.

What is included in a private campaign room booking?

Private room bookings include sound-dampened acoustic rooms, customizable LED lighting moods, built-in digital map displays, and full access to our modular terrain libraries.

Are store products available for immediate pickup or delivery?

Yes! All vault store items support immediate in-store pickup at our headquarters counter as well as standard home delivery across all domestic and regional zones.

Can I hire a Dungeon Master for a private group session?

Yes, we have veteran Dungeon Masters available for private bookings. You can select your preferred campaign setting, playstyle, and party difficulty when scheduling your session.

Are outside snacks and beverages allowed in the arena?

We offer a full-service snack bar with craft sodas, gourmet coffee, and gaming fuel snacks on-site. Outside drinks and light snacks are permitted in private lounges, but restricted near open gaming tables.
