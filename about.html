OUR ORIGINS

From a Single Basement Table to a Global Community Hub

Founded in 2021, PlayHaven started as a weekend gathering of friends frustrated by cramped spaces and poorly organized tournament calendars. We envisioned a venue where every detail—from custom sound-dampened private rooms to balanced lighting—enhances the game.

  • Built by veteran DMs, hobbyists, and competitive TCG players
  • Dedicated to preserving authentic face-to-face tabletop culture
  • Continuous expansion of modular terrain and rare game vaults
OUR GUIDING PRINCIPLES

The Three Pillars of PlayHaven

What drives every event we host and every product we curate.

Radical Inclusivity

Whether you're picking up dice for the first time or running your 500th campaign, our tables welcome all backgrounds and skill levels.

Boutique Quality

We reject flimsy components and chaotic rooms. Every seat offers pristine acoustics, custom terrain, and professional hosting.

Community First

We reinvest 20% of our event proceeds back into local prize pools, charity game days, and free youth introductory workshops.
